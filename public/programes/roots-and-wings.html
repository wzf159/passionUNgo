Community Knowledge Hub

Resources Shared by Families, for Families

Families often learn the most useful support through trusted conversations. Roots & Wings collects and shares local recommendations so more families can find help, friendship, and everyday support.

🍎

Food & Grocery Support

Food banks, pantry programs, WIC tips, affordable groceries, and family meal ideas shared by parents.

🏥

Healthcare & Family Wellness

Clinics, pediatric care, mental health support, insurance navigation, and parent wellbeing resources.

📚

Libraries & Early Learning

Story times, reading programs, homework help, free learning resources, and early childhood activities.

🎒

School & Education Resources

Preschool information, school readiness, after-school programs, tutoring, and parent education opportunities.

🌳

Parks & Family Activities

Playgrounds, parks, nature walks, beaches, museums, and low-cost weekend activities for families.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parenting Tips & Peer Support

Everyday parenting experiences, trusted advice, emotional support, and parent-to-parent encouragement.

🚌

Transportation & Access

Bus routes, parking tips, ride-share ideas, accessibility information, and ways to reach local services.

🌐

New-to-Community Support

Language-accessible services, cultural adjustment tips, local orientation, and resources for families new to Goleta/Santa Barbara.
